Working principles · Personal perspective

How I approach intelligent systems.

These are working principles, not institutional policy. They reflect how I currently think about responsible AI, human-centered design, and the practical realities of introducing intelligence into real workflows.

01 / AMPLIFY

Intelligence should amplify people.

Useful AI supports judgment, reduces avoidable friction, and gives people more room for meaningful work.

02 / TRUST

Trust is part of the architecture.

Clarity, accountability, security, and visible human control should be designed into the system from the beginning.

03 / EVIDENCE

Claims should be inspectable.

Research questions, methods, limitations, and evidence should remain distinguishable from personal interpretation.

04 / LEARN

Knowledge should compound.

Good systems preserve context, connect learning over time, and make useful discoveries easier to revisit and share.

Current investigations

Questions worth thinking about together.

These are open questions rather than finished conclusions. I share them to invite thoughtful disagreement, useful references, and better ideas.

?

How should an agent show its work?

What does useful transparency look like when an intelligent system uses tools, retrieves knowledge, or recommends an action?

?

How can organizational memory remain useful?

?
